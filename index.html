Featured Work

EVRA — swipe. shop. repeat.

A full Android clothing-retail app, designed and built solo for my final-year project. I gathered the requirements, modelled the Firestore database, sketched every screen, and shipped a working MVVM app end to end — registration through to checkout and order tracking.

Welcome screen

  • Firebase Authentication for secure registration and login, with Cloud Firestore modelling users, products, wishlists, carts and orders.
  • Full shopping journey — preference setup, category browsing, filtered search, wishlist, cart, checkout and order tracking with live status.
  • Built on MVVM to keep the codebase testable and easy to extend, developed in iterations using Agile.
